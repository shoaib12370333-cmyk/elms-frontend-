9Suspension, bans and ending

You can stop using ELMS at any time. We may suspend or end access if you break these Terms, put the service or other users at risk, or if we are required to by law or by a marketplace.

Suspension

  • A suspension is a block on your account. You cannot sign in or use ELMS while it lasts, your open sessions end, and listings that were scheduled to be published go back to drafts. Your drafts, listings and orders are kept, and we do not change anything that is already live on eBay.
  • We email you when your account is suspended. The email and the sign-in screen show the reason we gave.
  • You can appeal. Try to sign in: the blocked screen has an appeal form. Enter the email address of your account and tell us what happened. A person on our team reads every appeal and answers by email. An appeal does not lift a suspension by itself; we decide at our own discretion.

Permanent ban

  • For serious or repeated breaches, fraud, or risk to other people we may ban an account permanently. We email you when we do, and the email and the sign-in screen show the reason.
  • A permanent ban is final. The appeal form is not available for it. We may still reinstate an account at our own discretion. If we do, we tell you by email.
  • Unused credits may be forfeited when an account is ended for a breach of these Terms.

Blocked networks and browsers

We may block an IP address or a browser that is used for abuse. People who only share that connection and are not accused of anything can keep using ELMS, but new accounts from a blocked address are refused. If you are blocked, the blocked screen explains why and lets you send an appeal.

Getting around a suspension or ban

Creating a new account, or using another connection, to get around a suspension, a permanent ban or a block is a breach of these Terms. It can lead to a permanent ban of every account involved.

Emails about your account

We send emails about a suspension, a permanent ban, a reinstatement and the answer to an appeal to the address of your account. These are service emails, not marketing, so they are sent even if you switched off news and offers. See the Privacy Policy for how we use this data.
