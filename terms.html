The service

In brief

ELMS is a set of tools for turning supplier products into eBay listings and managing them. You stay the seller. You authorise ELMS to carry out the actions you set up or request on your connected accounts, including automatic ones. Many automatic features are switches you control.

What ELMS does

ELMS brings product information from supplier platforms you choose, mainly Amazon, into drafts. You can edit and improve the drafts, publish them to your eBay store, keep live listings up to date, follow orders and tracking, answer buyers and see the profit on your orders. You work in the web app and, for importing, in the ELMS browser extension for Chrome. The user guide describes each feature.

What you authorise ELMS to do

You authorise ELMS to carry out, on your connected accounts, the actions you set up or request, including automatic ones. ELMS acts only through the access you give it and only on the stores and accounts you connect. For example, ELMS may:

  • publish a draft at the time you chose, and try again when a publish fails;
  • end a listing, or change its price or its quantity, when stock and price monitoring calls for it (see Stock and price monitoring);
  • change many listings at once with a bulk tool that you start;
  • send a buyer message or a reply for you, when you turn automatic messages or automatic replies on;
  • bring orders, tracking, messages and listing details from the marketplace into ELMS, and send tracking details and order notes back to it.

You remain the seller and you are responsible for every listing, order and message that results. Many automatic features are switches that you can turn off. Stock and price monitoring starts on for each listing that has a source link, and you can switch it off listing by listing. ELMS does not undo a change it has already made on a marketplace. To reverse it, change it back yourself; to stop it happening again, switch the feature off.

AI help

ELMS uses AI services to help write and improve titles, descriptions, item details and message drafts. You decide what is published or sent: you review drafts before they go live, bulk actions ask you to confirm first, and automatic replies stay off until you turn them on.

AI output can be wrong, incomplete or too close to other text. Read it before you publish or send it, and check names, claims and brand words.

Some steps use AI output without a separate review of each result:

  • an AI tool that you start on live listings saves its result to eBay when you confirm it, so check your selection before you confirm;
  • when you publish a draft that has no eBay category, ELMS can pick one for it with the help of AI; and
  • automatic replies send a drafted reply on their own. They stay off until you turn them on.

The AI services we use process the text they are asked to work on, which can include a buyer’s message when you turn on reply help or automatic replies. The Privacy Policy explains how.

Stock and price monitoring

ELMS can watch the source products of your listings: when the source clearly says a product is out of stock, ELMS can end the listing for you, and when the source price changes it can update your eBay price to follow it. Both are switches you control per listing.

Monitoring starts on for each listing that has a source link. Stock monitoring can also set the quantity of a monitored listing. Switch it off on a listing to set the quantity yourself.

Monitoring uses information from the source site, which can be late, missing or wrong. A monitoring result is not a promise that a product is in stock or that a price is right.

The browser extensions

The ELMS product extension for Chrome runs in your own browser, on the supplier pages you open. It shows information about the product and lets you send the product to your ELMS drafts. It works with a personal extension key that you find in Settings. Keep the key private: anyone who has it can send products to your account. You can create a new key at any time, and the old key stops working at once. The sites you open may have rules about copying or reusing their content, and following them is your responsibility.

A second extension, the order helper, works on Amazon pages. It shows the delivery details of one of your orders so that you can copy them yourself. After you finish the purchase yourself, it reads the order total and the estimated delivery date from the confirmation page and saves them on the ELMS order. It does not click or type for you. The Privacy Policy describes the data both extensions use.
