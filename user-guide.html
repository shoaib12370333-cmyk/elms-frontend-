Quick start

Six steps from a new account to your first listing. Buttons and tabs are written as the app shows them, for example Publish.

  1. Create your account and confirm your e-mail with the code ELMS sends you (see how to create your account).
  2. Connect your eBay store. You approve the connection on eBay’s own page.
  3. Save your selling defaults: your payment, fulfillment and return policies and your item location.
  4. Set your margin, so ELMS adds your fees and profit to a product’s cost. Without a margin, a draft is priced at the Amazon price.
  5. Import a product from an Amazon link. It arrives as a draft in Drafts.
  6. Check the draft in the listing editor, then publish it.

Imports and publishing use credits. Your balance is the credits chip in the top bar; if it is empty, see Credits and plans before you import. If something goes wrong, see Troubleshooting.

Getting started

Create your account, connect your eBay store, save your selling defaults and choose the store you work in.

Create your account

Open the sign-up page. If you see the login form, choose Sign Up below it.

  1. Fill in Username, Email, Password and Confirm password.
  2. Pick the country of your phone number and type the number in Phone number. ELMS uses it for account contact and security.
  3. If a friend invited you, enter their code in Referral code. The field exists only at sign-up.
  4. Tick the box that accepts the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Refund Policy, then press Sign Up.
  5. Type the 6-digit code from your e-mail in Confirmation code and press Confirm and create my account.

No account exists until you enter the code. If the e-mail is slow, check spam and use Resend code; if you mistyped the address, choose Use another email.

Sign in, and other ways in

  • Email or username. Type it in Email or username, add your password and press Login.
  • Continue with eBay. Choose your eBay site beside the button, press Continue with eBay and approve on eBay. Back in ELMS, choose a username and password and give your e-mail and phone. Your store connects when you enter the e-mail code.
  • Sign-in provider. A button on the same form signs you in without typing a password.
  • Forgot password. Press Forgot password?, enter your e-mail and press Send code. Enter the Verification code and a New password, then press Reset password.

Connect your eBay store

You approve the connection on eBay’s own page, so ELMS does not see your eBay password.

  1. In the sidebar, under eBay account, press + Connect eBay account.
  2. In Choose your eBay marketplace, pick the eBay site your listings go to and press Continue to eBay.
  3. Sign in on eBay and approve. eBay lists the exact permissions on its own page; ELMS asks only for what its features need.
  4. Back in ELMS, your store appears under eBay stores in Settings.

Save your selling defaults

  1. Open Settings and find Selling settings.
  2. Under Editing settings for, choose the store and press Fetch from eBay.
  3. Choose your Payment policy, Fulfillment policy, Return policy and Product location.
  4. Press Save settings.

All four are needed to publish

If a list says none were found, create that policy in your eBay account, then press Fetch from eBay again.

Select, rename or disconnect a store

ELMS shows one store at a time, the one chosen under eBay account in the sidebar. Drafts, Live listings, Orders and Messages follow it. Switching deletes nothing.

  • Add another. Press + Add eBay account. How many stores you can connect comes with your plan.
  • Rename. Type a name on the store’s row in Settings and press Save name.
  • Disconnect. Disconnect hides the store. Its live listings, orders, messages and notes are kept for 30 days, then deleted; drafts stay and eBay is untouched. Reconnect the same eBay account within 30 days and everything returns.

Account basics

In the top bar, the bell opens your notifications, the credits chip shows your balance and opens Buy Credits (see Credits and plans), and your name opens a menu with Connect eBay, Settings and Logout. To change your phone number, press Change on the Phone number card in Settings.

Find your way around

The sidebar lists Dashboard, Import, Drafts, Live listings, Orders, Net Profit and Messages. Under Tools you find Research Tools and Price Calculator. Under Account you find Settings, Vouchers, Refer & Earn, Affiliate, Buy Credits and Support.

The Dashboard opens first. It shows Profit, Total revenue, Orders and New products, a Net Profit strip with buttons for the period, your Recent publish activity and your latest notifications. Open the sheet goes to Net Profit.

Importing

ELMS turns a product link into a draft with title, pictures, description, item details and a price from your margin. Nothing reaches eBay until you publish.

Import one product from a link

  1. Open Import. Stay on the Amazon tab and choose Single link.
  2. Paste the product page address into Amazon product link.
  3. Press Fetch product.

The field clears, so you can paste the next link at once. ELMS fetches in the background, tells you when the draft is ready and shows it in Drafts. Credits are used when the import completes. Your Margin prices the draft. If you have not set one, press Margin on this page before you import: without a margin, ELMS prices the draft at the Amazon price. See Pricing rule and margin.

  • One link, one draft. ELMS imports the product you linked. For another colour or size, import its own link.
  • The Amazon site must fit your store. A UK store takes amazon.co.uk links, a US store amazon.com links, and so on. If a link does not fit, ELMS says so and imports nothing.

Import a list of links

Both tabs take one Amazon link per line in Amazon links.

  • Bulk import suits a short list. Press Fetch all and keep the tab open. ELMS reports which links became drafts and why others failed.
  • Large import suits a long list. Press Start background import. A bar shows progress and you can close the tab; reopen Large import and the bar returns. Cancel keeps the drafts already saved, Retry failed repeats the failed links and View drafts opens the results.

Large import skips any line that is not a valid Amazon link.

Import from a CSV file

Use a CSV file for products without an Amazon link, such as your own stock. Each row becomes a draft. You need a connected store, because ELMS reads the Cost in your store’s currency.

  1. Open Import and choose the CSV file tab.
  2. Press Download template, delete its two example rows and add one row per product.
  3. Save as CSV and pick the file with Choose a CSV file, or drop it on the box. ELMS checks every row and lists what to fix. Nothing is saved yet.
  4. Press Import N products (the button shows how many rows passed the check) and keep the tab open. Stop ends it early; choose the same file again to continue, and imported products are updated, not doubled.

Add the pictures

Pictures come from the Image links or from your computer: in Drafts, press Upload images and name each file after its draft’s SKU. MUG-001.jpg is the first picture and MUG-001_2.jpg the second. ELMS shows which draft each file matches before it uploads. A draft without a picture cannot be published; No picture in Drafts lists them.

A CSV product has no source link, so ELMS leaves its stock and price monitoring off and you decide when to reprice or end it.

Import before you connect a store

You can start before your store is connected. The product becomes a draft that belongs to you, and it shows in Drafts either way. When you connect a store, ELMS attaches these drafts to it, so connect one before you publish. If ELMS asks for a store first, it imports nothing and uses no credit. A CSV import needs a store.

Drafts and the listing editor

Every imported product waits in Drafts until you publish it. Check it, change what you want, then publish. Publishing has its own section.

The Drafts page

Each product is a card with its picture, title, source, Cost, Sell price, Qty and Profit.

  • Stages. Ready to publish, Publishing now, Scheduled and Needs attention filter the list. A failed publish shows its reason.
  • Quick edits. Change Sell price or Qty on the card and the profit updates. When your margin priced the draft, Profit is what is left after eBay fees; otherwise it is sell price minus cost. With Stock Monitoring on, ELMS sets the quantity of an Amazon listing itself once it is live; see Monitoring stock and price.
  • Find. Search by title, ASIN or SKU, sort, filter by source, and switch between Cards and List. No picture and VeRO words show the drafts that need work.
  • Card buttons. Edit, Publish, and a three-dot menu with Schedule listing and Delete draft. + Add note keeps a private note; ELMS does not send it to eBay or Amazon.

The listing editor, tab by tab

Press Edit, or click the picture or title. Set Sell price and Quantity at the top. The draft saves itself as you work, and the footer says All changes saved.

ELMS suggests categories from your title and lists them in the Category box. Choose one before you publish; the item specifics load from it. The counter beside Title shows how much of eBay’s title space you have used.

AI helpers

ELMS uses AI services to help write and improve titles, descriptions, item details and message drafts. You decide what is published or sent: you review drafts before they go live, bulk actions ask you to confirm first, and automatic replies stay off until you turn them on.

  • Optimize Title With AI (Product tab) rewrites your title using the category and description.
  • Generate Description With AI writes a clean description from the product’s facts. Beautify With AI restructures the text in the box into your Description Template.
  • Fill with AI (Item Specifications) fills the empty item specifics it can answer and keeps what you typed. Choose a category first.

AI helpers can use credits when they finish, and your balance in the top bar updates. Read the result: AI can make mistakes. A few steps use AI output without a separate look at each result, so know where they are:

  • The AI tools in Live listings change listings that are already on eBay. They push their result to eBay when you confirm, so check your selection and read the window first.
  • When you publish a draft that has no eBay category, ELMS can pick one for it with the help of AI and save it on the draft.
  • Auto-send in Messages sends drafted replies on its own. It stays off until you turn it on; see AI drafts and reply modes.

Policies

Every listing needs a payment, a shipping (fulfillment) and a return policy, created in your eBay account. Your store defaults live in Selling settings. For one product, switch off Use Dynamic Policies (Shipping, Payment, Returns) on the Product tab and choose Payment Policy, Shipping Policy and Return Policy; Account default keeps the store default. For many drafts, tick Policies in Bulk edit.

Item location and postal code

eBay needs the place the item ships from, with a full postal code: in the UK the whole code, such as M1 1AE. Countries without postal codes, such as Hong Kong, need none.

  1. In Selling settings, open Product location. Choose a location from eBay, or Custom location…, the last entry of the list.
  2. For a custom location, choose the Country, type the City and press Generate valid code. ELMS suggests a postal code for that city. Use a code that matches where the item ships from. If you know your own, type it in Postal code and press Use my own code; ELMS checks it.
  3. Press Save settings.

One product can ship from elsewhere. On its Product tab, set Country Location, City and Zip code, or press Generate. A country and code saved on a product replace the store location for it.

Pricing rule and margin

Your margin turns a product’s cost into an eBay price, and every new import follows it. eBay takes its fees from what the buyer pays in total, so the margin adds your fees and profit to the cost.

  1. Press Margin on the Import page or in Settings. Switch on Use this margin for new imports and pick the currency in Amounts are in.
  2. Enter your fees in Fees % and Fixed fee per order (Help me calculate opens the Price Calculator), and your profit in Additional profit % and Additional profit. Add Minimum profit per product for a floor.
  3. Optional: Include shipping price adds delivery to the cost, Set price cents value rounds prices up to those cents, and Use different profit for cost ranges sets a profit per cost range (+ Add a range).
  4. Type a cost under Try your rule to see the price and its breakdown. Press Save pricing.

A rule applies to products you import from now on; drafts keep their price. To reprice drafts, use Bulk edit, tick Price and choose Use my pricing rule. With no margin, or with it off, products come in at the Amazon price.

Price Calculator

Price Calculator in the sidebar, under Tools, finds the price that leaves the profit you want after eBay’s fees, or shows what a price you already have earns you. Fill in What it costs you, eBay fees and What you want, or type a price under Or test a price you have. Price one of your drafts uses a draft’s Amazon cost and saves the result straight back to that draft.

Description template

In Settings, open Description Template. Pick a Starting style, tick the Blocks to include and add your branding: Store name (shown in the description), Logo URL (optional) and Accent color. You can add a Custom HTML block (optional), a Size chart HTML (optional) and a Product video URL (optional). Press Save. Beautify With AI and Beautify descriptions with AI use it.

Brand-word (VeRO) warnings

VeRO is eBay’s programme for brand owners to report listings that carry their names. eBay can remove such a listing and suspend accounts that repeat it. ELMS helps you check: you keep a list of words, and ELMS warns you when a draft or live listing uses one.

  1. In Settings, find the VeRO words card and press VeRO words.
  2. Type a word and press Enter, or paste several separated by commas. Use ELMS’s words adds a suggested list, Import from PDF adds the usable words of a PDF list and Clear all empties yours. Only words you save are used.

A draft with one of your words shows a VeRO chip, and the editor shows VeRO words found. The check covers the whole listing text, item specifics and brand. The VeRO words button in Drafts lists those drafts.

Press Remove brand words on the card, in the editor or in the bulk bar. Each time, ELMS asks you to tick that taking a brand word out of the text does not give you the right to sell a branded item; without the tick nothing is removed. ELMS removes the words and names them, so open the draft to check. It changes text only, not pictures. Every word of your list is removed, also one used in its ordinary meaning, and a sentence that no longer reads well may be dropped, so check the result. Publishing a draft that still has VeRO words asks you to confirm first; see Publish one draft.

Warnings are aids, not clearance

ELMS can warn only about the words you saved, and eBay can still act on a listing that passes. Taking a brand word out of the text does not make a branded or counterfeit item allowed. You are the seller, and you are responsible for what you list. For items eBay prohibits outright, see The prohibited-items stop.

Publishing

Publishing turns a draft into a live eBay listing in your selected store. You can publish one draft, publish a selection, or set a time for a draft to go live. ELMS works in the background, so you can keep using the app.

In brief

Press Publish on a draft, or select drafts and press Publish all. Credits are used when a listing is published and come back if the publish fails.

Before you publish

Publishing needs a payment policy, a fulfillment policy, a return policy and an item location for the store. You set them once in Selling settings; the steps are in Save your selling defaults. To use other policies or another location for one draft, see Policies and Item location and postal code.

Each draft also needs a selling price, an eBay category with the item specifics it requires, and at least one picture. A draft without a picture is marked No picture. If a draft has no category when you publish it, ELMS can pick one with the help of AI and save it on the draft; choose your own in the editor if you want to decide.

Publish one draft

  1. Open Drafts and check the card: Cost, Sell price, Qty and the profit line.
  2. Press Publish. If the draft contains VeRO words, ELMS shows VeRO words found. Choose Publish anyway or Go back.
  3. The draft is counted under Publishing now. When eBay accepts it, you get a notification and the listing appears in Live listings.

Publish many drafts

  1. Tick the drafts, as described in Work on many drafts at once.
  2. Press Publish all in the bar at the bottom and confirm.
  3. ELMS publishes in the background. A progress line at the top of Drafts shows how far it has got, and a notification tells you when the batch is done.

Drafts that eBay does not allow and drafts with no picture are left out, and ELMS tells you about them. If some drafts contain VeRO words, you confirm once for the selection.

Schedule a draft

  1. On the card open the three-dot menu (More actions) and choose Schedule listing.
  2. Choose the store in Publish to (hidden when you have one store), set Publish date & time and press Schedule.

The card moves to Scheduled and shows when it goes live, in your computer’s time. ELMS publishes at that time or shortly after, with the same checks as a normal publish, and credits are used then. Press Cancel schedule on the card to send the draft back to draft.

When a publish fails

A publish can fail because of the draft or because of eBay, for example a missing policy, a required item specific, no usable picture or a store that needs to be reconnected. Then:

  • the credits come back automatically;
  • the card shows Failed with the reason, and the draft is counted under Needs attention;
  • you get a Publish failed notification.

If eBay answers with a temporary error, ELMS tries again on its own before it reports a failure. To publish a failed draft again:

  1. Press Retry on the card. ELMS clears the failure and opens the Edit draft window.
  2. Fix what the reason names. In this window you can add a picture with Add images or Add from URL, set the eBay category ID or correct the Sell price, then press Save changes. If the reason is a policy or the item location, close the window, press Edit on the card and use the Product tab (see Policies and Item location and postal code).
  3. Open Drafts again, or reload the page, so the card refreshes. Then press Publish.

The prohibited-items stop

eBay prohibits some products outright. Before a draft goes to eBay, ELMS checks its text against its own list of those products. On a match the card shows Not allowed on eBay with the area it matched, and ELMS will not publish it. Nothing is sent to eBay and nothing is charged. A window explains why when you press Publish, and Publish all leaves those drafts out and offers to publish the rest. If you think a match is a mistake, change the title or description, or write to support.

An aid, not clearance

The stop and the VeRO warnings are aids, not clearance; see Brand-word (VeRO) warnings.

Publishing again

A sold-out or ended listing goes back on eBay with Relist. See Relist sold-out and ended listings.

Live listings

Live listings shows what you published to eBay from the selected store: price, profit, stock and watchers. Boxes above the list add up the store, for example Live now, Units sold, Watchers, Average margin and Need attention.

Sync from eBay

Press Sync from eBay to refresh the numbers that come from eBay, such as watchers. A line beside the button says when they were last synced; ELMS also refreshes them on its own. Edit reads the listing’s current details from eBay first, and Save changes updates the listing on eBay.

Find your listings

The tabs are All, Active, Sold, Ended and Issues, each with a count. Issues holds listings that need you, and the red box says why. If eBay reports live listings that break a rule, a banner above the list names the problems. Search by title, SKU or item ID, sort, filter by source or by VeRO words, switch between Cards and List, and press Export CSV to download the view. Change many listings at once

Tick the Select box to take in the page, then press Select all N to take in the whole view. You can also tick listings one by one. The bar offers what fits the tab:

  • Change price (Active tab) reprices the selection on eBay with Use my pricing rule or numbers you enter, from each listing’s own Amazon price. A listing without one is skipped, with the reason.
  • Restock (Sold tab) puts a new quantity on eBay.
  • Bulk edit lets you tick what to change: Price, Title, Brand, Quantity, Tags, Note, Stock monitoring, Price monitoring, Item location or Policies. Price, title, brand, quantity, location and policies go to eBay; tags, note and monitoring stay in ELMS. ELMS asks you to confirm and lists every listing it skipped, with the reason.
  • Fill specifics with AI, Beautify descriptions with AI and Remove brand words push their result to eBay and can use credits. Read the window before you confirm.
  • Fit titles to eBay cuts a title that ends mid-word back to the last whole word. Only the title changes, and no credits are used.

Relist sold-out and ended listings

Select listings and press Relist, or use the three-dot menu on an ended listing. A window first shows what happens to each kind: a live listing is left as it is, a sold-out listing gets the quantity you type, an ended listing is published again as a new listing, and a listing that needs attention is not touched. Tick Also relist the other listings to include the rest of the store. Relisting an ended listing goes through the publishing steps above, so it uses credits like any publish.

End and delete

Select listings and press Delete & end on eBay, or choose Delete in the three-dot menu of one listing. ELMS ends a live listing on eBay and then removes it from ELMS. You confirm first, and it cannot be undone. On the Ended and Issues tabs the button reads Delete.

Monitoring stock and price

ELMS can watch the source products of your listings: when the source clearly says a product is out of stock, ELMS can end the listing for you, and when the source price changes it can update your eBay price to follow it. Both are switches you control per listing.

What monitoring does

  • Stock. ELMS looks at the Amazon product the listing was created from. If it clearly says the product is out of stock, ELMS ends the listing on eBay. While the source shows the product in stock, ELMS keeps the listing available on eBay and sets the eBay quantity itself, and a listing that sold out can be put back on eBay the same way. To set the quantity yourself, switch Stock Monitoring off for that listing.
  • Price. When the source price moves, ELMS recalculates your eBay price. A listing that was priced with your Margin keeps the rule it was priced with, so changing the Margin card later does not change it. A listing priced without a rule keeps the same gap between its source price and its eBay price. To price a live listing again with your current Margin, use Change price in Live listings and choose Use my pricing rule.
  • Credits. Each check uses credits, whichever of the two switches is on. Switch both off to stop them. A check with no clear answer is not charged: the credit is returned. If your balance cannot cover a check, ELMS skips it, ends nothing and sends you a notification.

A listing is watched through the supplier link it was created from. A product added from a CSV file has no such link, so there is nothing to watch.

Switch it on or off for a listing

Both switches start on, so monitoring uses credits when it runs even if you click nothing. If you switch both off, ELMS leaves that listing alone.

  1. Open Live listings and press Edit on the listing. For a draft, press Edit on its card in Drafts; the switches apply once it is published.
  2. On the Product tab go to Monitoring.
  3. Switch Stock Monitoring and Price Monitoring on or off, then press Save changes.

For many listings, select them, press Bulk edit, tick Stock monitoring or Price monitoring, and choose On or Off in Watch Amazon stock or Follow Amazon price changes.

What you see when ELMS acts

When ELMS ends a listing because the source is out of stock, you get a notification and the listing moves to the Ended tab. Relist it when the product is back. When the price changes, the new selling price and cost show on the listing.

What monitoring does not do

  • Monitoring ends a listing only when the source clearly says the product is out of stock. If the source does not answer, or does not say whether the product is in stock, the listing stays as it is.
  • Monitoring does not relist a listing on the Ended tab. You do that yourself.
  • Monitoring does not order anything from the source for you.
  • Monitoring does not change a title, a description or pictures. It can only end a listing, change its quantity or change its price.
  • Monitoring leaves alone a listing whose two switches are off, and a store that needs to be reconnected.

Good to know

ELMS acts on what the source shows when it checks. Source data can be late or wrong, so keep an eye on your orders.

Orders and tracking

Orders lists the eBay orders ELMS has loaded for the selected store, with their payment, address, tracking and your notes. New and changed orders arrive on their own. Press Sync from eBay to look right away; the first time, it loads your recent orders. Full sync re-reads all recent orders, not only the changes.

Find and group your orders

The tabs group orders by where they stand: All, Awaiting shipment, Awaiting payment, Shipped, Delivered and Cancelled, each with a count. The cards on top add up the period you chose in the box beside the search: orders awaiting shipment, revenue, profit and cancelled orders. On Awaiting shipment a filter shows Not ordered yet or Ordered. You can search by order ID, buyer, item or tracking number.

Work through an order

  1. Press Fulfil on the row (View for other orders). The order window shows the item, payment, the address with Copy address, fulfillment and your private note, plus View on eBay, Refresh from eBay, Message buyer and Packing slip.
  2. Buy the item from your source yourself. The AMAZON label on the row opens the product, and the order helper extension can help at this step. ELMS never places Amazon orders.
  3. Mark the order as ordered, then as shipped.

Mark as ordered

  1. On an order awaiting shipment, open the arrow beside the status (Order actions) and choose Mark as ordered.
  2. Enter the Delivery date (when the parcel arrives) and your Buying price. Both are optional.
  3. Press Mark as ordered.

The row shows Ordered with the arrival date. It is only a reminder for you: nothing goes to eBay or Amazon unless you switch on the eBay note below or the ordered message in Settings. ELMS adds ELMS: ordered and the date to the order’s private note, and the buying price goes to the Net Profit sheet. To remove the mark choose Undo “ordered” mark. If you switched on the ordered message under Auto buyer messages in Settings, ELMS sends it the first time you mark the order.

You can also type the buying price on the order itself. The Buying cost box on the row and Buying price in the order window are the same figure as Buying price on the Net Profit sheet. Cost per item in the order window is separate: it only sets the profit shown on Orders. Amazon order ID is a field for your own record; saving it also marks the order as ordered.

The order helper extension

The order helper is a browser extension for Chrome; it shows its name as ELMS Order Sync (search the Chrome Web Store for that name; if you cannot find it, open a Support ticket and we will send you the link). It works on Amazon pages, and only in a tab you opened with the AMAZON label on an order row. It never clicks, types or submits anything for you: you place the order yourself.

  • A small panel, ELMS buyer address, shows the buyer’s delivery details from the order. Press Copy beside a field, or Copy full address, and paste it into Amazon’s own form yourself.
  • After you buy, press Mark as ordered in the same panel (on Amazon’s order confirmation page the panel shows only this button). Type the Delivery date and your Buying price, both optional, and press Mark as ordered again. ELMS marks the order exactly as when you use Mark as ordered in ELMS: the same note, the same ordered message to the buyer if you switched it on, and the note on eBay if you switched that on. The panel tells you what happened, also on eBay. To take the mark away again, use Undo “ordered” mark in ELMS.
  • It does not read what is on Amazon’s pages. It saves only what you type, and only when you press the button.

It connects with your personal extension key. Treat the key like a password: anyone who has it can use your account through the extension, and Regenerate makes a new key and stops the old one at once. Copy the key from the Browser extension card in Settings, paste it into ELMS Extension Key in the helper’s popup and press Connect ELMS Account. There is no separate switch for it in Settings. Disconnect ELMS in the popup removes the key from the browser.

Mark as shipped and add tracking

  1. Open Order actions and choose Mark as shipped, or press it in the order window.
  2. Choose the Carrier and type the Tracking number. If your carrier is not listed, choose Other.
  3. Press Mark as shipped.

The tracking number is optional. With one, ELMS sends it to eBay and the buyer sees it there. Without one, the order is marked shipped on eBay with no tracking number, and Update tracking adds it later. If eBay refuses the update, ELMS says Saved in ELMS only and shows the reason. A shipped message you switched on in Settings is sent now, once per order.

The buyer tracking page

When you save a tracking number, the order window shows a link to a tracking page for the buyer, with Copy link. The page shows a general status of the parcel. You can share the link with the buyer.

The order note

Every order has a private note. It is not shown to your buyer or to other ELMS users. Press + Add note on the row, or use Private note in the order window, and save. It stays in ELMS. Two switches above the list can also write to the private note of the order on eBay. Both are off until you switch them on, and ELMS asks you to confirm.

  • Also write “ordered” in the eBay note adds ELMS: ordered and the delivery date to eBay’s private note when you mark an order. Your own text there is kept.
  • Also copy my order note to eBay puts the note you save into eBay’s private note and replaces what is there. An empty note clears it. eBay keeps only a short note, so a long one is cut.

The eBay note can be written only while eBay lists the order as awaiting shipment.

Delivered

Press Mark delivered in the order window when the parcel has arrived. ELMS also moves a shipped order to Delivered once eBay’s estimated delivery date has passed. An order with no estimated date stays in Shipped until you mark it.

Buyer messages

Messages gathers the buyer conversations of the selected store in one inbox. You read and answer buyers, keep private notes and reuse saved replies. ELMS can also draft replies and send a few automatic messages. It sends nothing to a buyer unless you press Send, or you switched on Auto-send or an automatic buyer message.

The inbox

The page follows the store chosen in the sidebar. New buyer messages arrive in the inbox on their own, and Sync looks right away.

  • Tabs. From buyers, From eBay (notices from eBay itself) and Needs reply. The box under them switches between Inbox, Unread only, Archived and Trash. You can sort and search.
  • Rows. Unread conversations are bold. A tag such as AI draft shows what ELMS prepared. Buyer unknown means eBay did not name the recipient.
  • Many at once. Tick several rows to Mark as read or Archive them.

Read and reply

  1. Select a conversation. When eBay links it to an order, View order details opens Orders.
  2. Type in the box at the bottom. The buttons beside it attach a file or a photo and add an emoji.
  3. Press Send. ELMS sends the reply through eBay.

The three-dot menu of a thread offers Archive, Mark unread and Move to Trash; from Trash you can Restore it.

Saved replies

Press Saved replies at the top to add a reusable reply or delete one. In a conversation, choose it from the Saved replies list and it fills the box for you to edit. To keep a reply you just wrote, press Save reply and name it. Placeholders such as {{buyer_name}} and {{order_id}} are filled in when you use the reply.

Private notes

Open Private note, write it and press Add note. A note is private. It is not sent to the buyer and other ELMS users do not see it.

AI drafts and reply modes

The AI replies bar has three modes. The note beside it says how many credits a reply uses.

  • Off: you write every reply. Draft with AI in a conversation still gives you one draft.
  • Draft for me: when a new buyer message arrives, ELMS puts a draft in the reply box and tags the row AI draft. Edit it and press Send, or press Discard.
  • Auto-send: after you confirm Turn on Auto-send?, ELMS answers messages that look simple without asking. It tries to keep messages about refunds, returns, damage and complaints as drafts for you, but it cannot catch every wording, so switch Auto-send on only if you accept that a reply can go out before you have read it. The row gets the tag AI replied, and the thread shows what was sent.

Only messages that arrive after you switch a mode on are handled. If a draft cannot be written, the credits come back. If your balance is too low, ELMS skips the draft and notifies you.

Check before you send

AI can make mistakes. Check names, dates and promises. Automatic replies stay off until you turn them on.

Credits and plans

Credits are used when an action completes. If an action fails, the credits come back automatically. A plan is paid for one term; the credits that come with a plan are valid until the term ends, and we e-mail you before it ends. Credits you had before a plan are not affected.

Where to see your balance, and where a cost is shown

Your balance is shown at the top of the app: the credits chip in the top bar shows it and opens Buy Credits, which also shows Your balance. Some windows show the cost before you confirm, and a few other places show it beside the action:

  • A window that asks you to confirm a bulk action, such as filling categories, item details or descriptions with AI for a selection, states the cost first. An item that is skipped or fails is not charged.
  • On Messages, the note beside the AI replies modes says what a reply uses.

Other actions, such as an import from the Import page, a publish, a monitoring check or a research lookup, use credits when they complete, and the credits chip shows your new balance.

When your balance is too low, ELMS stops and shows Out of credits with a Buy credits button. Nothing you built is lost.

  2. If you use the order helper extension, open Settings, find the Browser extension card and press Regenerate. A new password does not end the extension key; regenerating stops the old key at once. Paste the new key into the extension.

Troubleshooting

Find the message you see. If a fix does not work, open a Support ticket and include the exact message.

An import fails

If you see That does not look like a valid Amazon product URL. paste the product page address itself. If ELMS says the link is from a different Amazon site, use a link from the site that fits the selected store. If you see ELMS could not reach the product source right now. Nothing was charged. try again in a moment.

A store needs reconnecting

A red banner says Reconnect your eBay store. and names what is not coming in, such as new orders or buyer messages. Press Reconnect eBay and agree on eBay’s page. Your listings and data stay as they are.

A publish fails

The card shows Failed with the reason, and the credits come back. Press Retry, fix what the reason names and save, then open Drafts again and press Publish. See When a publish fails.

Credits seem to be missing

Credits from a failed action come back on their own, normally within moments. Reload the page and check the credits chip. A plan’s credits end with its term. If one is still missing, open a ticket with the topic Credits and name the action and the time.

I paid and my plan is not active

Stay on Buy Credits for a moment. ELMS adds the plan once the payment is confirmed and then tells you. If nothing changes, open a ticket with the topic Billing and give the date and amount.

A check cannot run right now

The message This check cannot run right now. Please try again later. means the check could not run. Try again later. If an action fails, its credits come back.

Orders or messages do not show up

Check that the right store is selected in the sidebar, because every page follows it, and look for the reconnect banner. Then press Sync from eBay on Orders or Sync on Messages. Still missing? Open a ticket with the topic Orders / messages.

Order earning stays empty in Net Profit

A dash means eBay has not paid that order out yet. If it stays empty for paid-out orders, look at your store under eBay stores in Settings. A chip reading Order earning isn’t syncing for this store has a Reconnect eBay button.

The AI helper is not available

If you see The AI helper is not available right now. Please try again in a moment. No credits were used. try again shortly or write the text yourself.

I cannot sign in

Press Forgot password? to set a new password. A blocked screen gives the reason and, where it applies, an appeal form; see Suspension, bans and appeals.
