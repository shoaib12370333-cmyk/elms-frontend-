How it works

From product link to live listing in four steps

You decide what is published or sent. ELMS takes on the slow parts.

  1. Connect your eBay store

    Sign in with eBay and approve access on eBay's own page, so your eBay password stays with eBay. Then choose the store you want to work in. If your plan allows more than one store, you can connect more later.

  2. Add product links

    Paste one Amazon link or a whole list. Use the Chrome extension while you browse Amazon, or import a CSV file. Each product becomes a draft in ELMS, ready for you to review.

  3. Review and polish

    Check the title, pictures, item specifics, price and policies. Ask AI to help with the wording. Your pricing rule sets the starting price, and brand-word warnings appear before you publish.

  4. Publish and monitor

    Publish now or choose a time. After that, ELMS can monitor stock and price on the product page each listing came from, and your orders and buyer messages come into the same place.

What you need to start

  • An eBay seller account, which you connect to ELMS.
  • Product links, mainly from Amazon, or a CSV file of your own products.
  • Your payment, shipping and return policies from eBay and an item location. Fetch the policies and set the location in Settings, once for each store.

Features

Tools for every stage, from import to delivery

Use the ones you need and leave the rest.

Import from a link, a list or a file

Paste one Amazon link or a whole list, or use the ELMS Chrome extension while you browse. Already have your products in a spreadsheet? Import a CSV file, with pictures from links or from your computer. Long lists import in the background, so you can close the tab.

Drafts with AI help

Edit the title, description, pictures, item specifics, policies and item location in one editor. AI can suggest titles, descriptions and item specifics. Pick a description template with your own branding, and AI can lay out your descriptions with it. You review drafts before they go live, and bulk actions ask you to confirm first.

Publish now, later or in batches

Send a draft live, publish many at once or schedule them for a time you pick. A failed publish shows its reason, so you can fix the draft and retry.

A pricing rule you set once

Set your profit goal and fees once under Margin in Settings, and ELMS prices your drafts with that rule. The price calculator and the Chrome extension show what a price leaves you after eBay's fees.

Live listings and bulk edit

See your live listings in one place and sync them from eBay. Change prices, titles, item locations and policies on many listings at once. Relist sold-out and ended listings, or end the ones you no longer want.

Stock and price monitoring

ELMS can watch the source products of your listings. When the source clearly says a product is out of stock, ELMS can end the listing for you. When the source price changes, it can update your eBay price to follow it. Both are switches you control per listing, and they start on for each listing that has a source link.

Keep orders and tracking together

Your eBay orders arrive grouped by stage, from awaiting payment to delivered. Mark an order as ordered or shipped, add a tracking number when you have one and ELMS passes it to eBay. A private note keeps the details with the order.

See profit order by order

On the Net Profit page, enter what you paid for each item. ELMS reads what eBay paid out when eBay provides it, and shows the result for any date range, with search and a spreadsheet export.

Buyer messages with saved replies

Read and answer buyer messages for the store you are working in. Use saved replies, add private notes and let AI draft an answer for you to check. Automatic messages, such as a thank-you or a shipping update, stay off until you turn them on.

Research before you list

Check where a product ranks for a keyword on Amazon, read what customers say in its reviews, explore categories and bestsellers, view product images and grade an Amazon listing, all before you commit to a product.

Know when something needs you

A bell in the app tells you when publishing has finished and when something needs your attention. E-mails warn you about sign-ins from a new device and remind you before a plan term ends.

Browser extension

Import while you browse Amazon

The ELMS Chrome extension adds a small panel to Amazon product pages, so you can judge a product before it becomes a draft.

  • See what a product would leave you after eBay's fees, before you import it.
  • Get warnings about brand words from your own list, and about other problems with the product.
  • Find out when a product is already in your Drafts or your live listings, for the store you chose.
  • Import with one click, then open the draft in ELMS.
  • On search and bestseller pages, tick several products and import them together.

After you create your account, open Settings and find Browser extension: your extension key is there. Treat the key like a password. The extension works in Chrome.

Stay in control

You decide. ELMS does the legwork.

ELMS uses AI services to help write and improve titles, descriptions, item details and message drafts. You decide what is published or sent: you review drafts before they go live, bulk actions ask you to confirm first, and automatic replies stay off until you turn them on.

  • Stock and price monitoring starts on for each listing that has a source link, and you can switch it off listing by listing. Automatic buyer messages stay off until you turn them on.

Automatic messages

Prohibited-items check

Before a draft goes to eBay, ELMS checks its text for items that eBay prohibits outright. A draft that matches is stopped, nothing is charged, and you see the reason. It is a safeguard, and it does not replace your own check.

Brand-word (VeRO) warnings

Keep your own list of brand words. ELMS flags drafts and live listings that contain one, can take the words out of the text for you after you tick a notice, and asks you to confirm before you publish a listing that still has one. Taking a word out does not make a branded item allowed to sell.

Aids, not clearance

These checks help you look. A missing warning does not mean an item or a word is allowed. You are the seller, and the final check is yours.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Short answers to what sellers ask first. The user guide has the detail.

How do credits work?

Credits are used when an action completes. If an action fails, the credits come back automatically.

A plan is paid for one term; the credits that come with a plan are valid until the term ends, and we e-mail you before it ends. Credits you had before a plan are not affected.

What happens when an action fails?

The credits come back to your balance automatically, normally within moments, and you do not need to ask. If one is missing, write to support@elmstool.com and we restore it. A failed publish also shows you the reason, so you can fix the draft and try again.

Do I need an eBay seller account?

Yes. You connect your own eBay seller account, which ELMS calls a store. A connection is needed to publish. You connect it by signing in on eBay and approving access on eBay's own page, so your eBay password stays with eBay. You choose which connected store you work in.

Which eBay sites and countries does ELMS work with?

You choose your eBay site when you set up your store. The list includes eBay US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Italy and Spain, among others.

Products come mainly from Amazon product links. You can also import a CSV file of your own products, and other supplier platforms can be connected. For many eBay sites the link has to come from the Amazon site of the same country, and ELMS tells you when a link does not match your store.

Does ELMS order from Amazon for me?

No. ELMS does not place Amazon orders. You buy the item yourself when an order comes in. In ELMS you can mark the order as ordered, note what you paid and keep your tracking details together with the order.

Who is responsible for my listings?

You are. ELMS is built for people who sell for business, and you are the seller: you decide what to list, and you are responsible for each listing, order and message. That includes following the rules of eBay and of the supplier sites you source from. ELMS gives you drafts, checks and warnings to help. They are aids, not approval.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, eBay and supplier access tokens are stored encrypted, and data is encrypted in transit. No system is completely secure.

Payments are handled by our payment partner. ELMS never sees your card or wallet details. The Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

Can I get a refund?

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of a purchase. You do not need to give a reason, and using credits does not stop a refund.

When a refund is approved, the plan and the credits that came with that purchase end. The Refund Policy explains how the 14 days are counted.

How do I contact you?

Write to support@elmstool.com for help with ELMS, to hello@elmstool.com for general questions before you sign up, to billing@elmstool.com for refunds and invoices, and to security@elmstool.com to report a security problem. When you are signed in, you can also open a support ticket from the Support page in the app. The Contact page says what to include in a message.

Is there a browser extension?

Yes. The ELMS Chrome extension adds a panel to Amazon product pages, where you check a product and import it into your Drafts with one click. You connect it with the extension key from your Settings page; the section above shows what the panel does.

Can I cancel or stop?

A plan is paid for one term, and its credits last until the term ends. If your payment method renews your plan, you can stop the renewal before the renewal date from your receipt or by writing to billing@elmstool.com. To close your account, write to support@elmstool.com.

What is the VeRO helper?

VeRO is eBay's programme that lets brand owners report listings that infringe their rights. In ELMS you keep your own list of brand words, and ELMS warns you when a draft or live listing contains one.

It helps you check. It is not clearance. Taking a brand word out of the text does not give you the right to sell a branded item, and the Remove brand words button asks you to confirm that every time you press it.
