Questions

Good to know

Do I need an eBay account?

Yes. You connect your own eBay seller account (or several) to ELMS with eBay's secure sign-in, and ELMS lists to it on your behalf. You can create your ELMS account first and connect eBay afterwards.

How do credits work?

Actions such as importing a product, publishing a listing or an AI touch-up use credits. You buy a pack when you need more, and credits do not expire on a schedule. If an action fails, the credits it used are returned.

Does ELMS order from Amazon for me?

No. ELMS prepares everything: it shows you the orders that need action, your cost and your profit, and sends the tracking number to eBay. You place the Amazon order yourself.

Can I import lots of products at once?

Yes. Paste a list of Amazon links and ELMS imports them in the background, so you can close the tab. You are charged per product that is actually imported. Links that fail, and repeats of the same product, are not charged.

Which Amazon and eBay sites are supported?

ELMS works with the main Amazon sites (US, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Canada, Australia and more) and the matching eBay marketplaces. ELMS checks that the Amazon link fits the store you are listing to, so you do not mix up countries.

Do I have to wait while it publishes?

No. Publishing, syncing messages and syncing orders run in the background, so you can keep working. You get a message when each one is done, and a listing that failed shows the reason on its card.

Does it work for UK and Australian stores?

Yes. Each store uses the matching Amazon site (amazon.co.uk for a UK store, amazon.com.au for an Australian one), prices stay in that store's currency, and postal codes are checked in that country's format. US, UK and Australian stores are the ones we look after most closely.

Is my account safe?

Sign-in is protected, you can see and log out of every device signed in to your account, and you get an alert when a new device signs in. For eBay, ELMS never asks for your eBay password: you connect through eBay itself.
