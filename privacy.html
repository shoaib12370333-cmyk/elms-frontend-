Privacy Policy

ELMS stores account information, listing data, order data, and connected eBay account authorization data needed to provide the service.

eBay data

When you connect eBay, ELMS uses the permissions you authorize to manage listings, orders, and messages. We do not sell your eBay account data.

Security

Authentication credentials and authorization tokens are protected using server-side controls. Never share your ELMS password or eBay authorization credentials.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ELMS service administrator.
