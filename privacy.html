Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ELMS ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our service, which helps sellers import Amazon product listings and publish them to eBay.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and (if you sign in with Google) your Google profile picture.
  • eBay account information: when you connect your eBay account, we store an encrypted access token so we can publish, edit, and manage listings on your behalf. We do not see or store your eBay password.
  • Listing and order data: product details you import from Amazon, the listings you publish to eBay, and order information (such as buyer username, sale price, and shipping status) synced from your eBay account.
  • Payment information: when you purchase credits, payment is processed by our payment provider, Paddle.com. We do not receive or store your card details — Paddle acts as the merchant of record for these transactions.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type and access times, used for security and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the core service: importing Amazon product data and publishing/managing eBay listings on your behalf.
  • To process credit purchases and maintain your account balance.
  • To monitor Amazon stock availability for your published listings and keep them accurate.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us or submit a support ticket.
  • To keep our service secure and prevent misuse.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with the following third-party services to operate ELMS:

  • Google — for sign-in authentication.
  • eBay — to publish, manage, and sync listings and orders on your behalf, via eBay's official APIs.
  • Amazon product data providers — to retrieve publicly available product information (title, price, images, description) for the items you choose to import.
  • Paddle.com — our payment provider and merchant of record, which processes credit card and other payments securely.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with the third parties listed above only as needed to provide the service (for example, sending your listing details to eBay to publish them, or your email to Paddle to process a payment).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and listing data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your eBay account, eBay notifies us automatically so we can remove the associated data from our systems. You may also request deletion of your ELMS account and data at any time by contacting us (see Section 8).

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage of eBay access tokens and hashed storage of account passwords. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to the best of our ability.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain uses of it. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out through the Support page in the ELMS app, or email us at the address associated with your account setup.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
