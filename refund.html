Refund Policy

We refund a purchase only if both of these are true: you ask within 7 days of the purchase, and none of that purchase’s credits have been used. If either one is not true, the purchase cannot be refunded under this policy.

When we refund

In brief

Refunds are given only if you ask within 7 days of the purchase and none of that purchase’s credits have been used. Both must be true.

We refund a purchase if, and only if, both of these are true:

  1. 1

    You ask within 7 days

    Your request reaches billing@elmstool.com or becomes a support ticket within 7 days of the purchase, counted from the moment the payment was confirmed. See how the 7 days are counted.

  2. and
  3. 2

    No credit from that purchase has been used

    None of the credits from that purchase have been used, by you, by a person on your team or by an automatic feature. See what “used” means.

If either one is not true, the purchase cannot be refunded under this policy.

How the 7 days are counted

In brief

The clock starts when your payment is confirmed. A request counts when it reaches billing@elmstool.com or becomes a support ticket.

Purchase date
The moment your payment was confirmed. We read it in UTC, the same way as on your invoice.
7 days
7 × 24 hours, counted from that moment.
A request counts
When it reaches billing@elmstool.com, or when it is created as a support ticket.

So a payment confirmed at 14:30 UTC on the 3rd can be refunded if your request reaches us before 14:30 UTC on the 10th, and none of the credits from that purchase have been used.

If you are not sure whether a purchase still qualifies, write to us before the 7 days end.

Send your request to billing@elmstool.com or open a ticket in Support. A request sent to another ELMS address counts from the time it reaches billing@elmstool.com or becomes a ticket. Our records of that time decide.

For a plan, your invoice shows the purchase date in UTC. We e-mail it after the payment, and you can download it again from Purchase history on the Buy Credits page. The invoice shows the day only. Purchase history shows the date and time in your own time zone. For a listing pack, the date is in Purchase history under Buy Listings, and a pack has no invoice. For the exact moment of a purchase, ask billing@elmstool.com.

What “used” means

In brief

A credit is used as soon as any action has been charged for it.

In this policy, a credit is used as soon as any action has been charged for it. It does not matter who started the action: you, a person on your team, or an automatic feature.

Some automatic features, such as stock and price monitoring, start on for each listing that has a source link and stay on until you switch them off. They can use credits when they run. So a purchase can count as used without you clicking anything. Scheduled publishing does the same once you schedule a listing.

Your credits sit in one balance. So a purchase counts as unused only if no credit has been charged to your account since the purchase. One charged import, publish, AI helper or monitoring check after the purchase is enough for the purchase to count as used, even if you also held other credits before it.

Credits that were charged and then returned because an action failed do not count as used. See Failed actions.

Failed actions

In brief

If an action fails, its credits are returned to your balance automatically. You do not need to ask.

When an action does not complete, the credits it was charged are returned to your balance automatically, normally within moments. If you think a credit is missing, write to support@elmstool.com or open a ticket in Support, and we restore it.

Returned credits are not a refund of money. They go back into your balance.

Plans, listing packs and one-off services

The two conditions apply to every kind of purchase. Each purchase has its own date and is checked on its own.

Plans and credits

A plan is paid for one term, and the credits that come with it are valid until the term ends. If you ask in time and no credit has been charged since the purchase, the plan can be refunded.

Listing packs

A listing pack adds drafts to your Drafts as soon as the payment is confirmed. Delivery is immediate, so a pack counts as used as soon as any part of it has been delivered. A pack that has not been delivered at all has not been used. If you received fewer drafts than you bought, write to billing@elmstool.com.

One-off services

A one-off service counts as used as soon as any part of it has been delivered. Before any delivery, the same two conditions apply.

What is not refundable

These cannot be refunded under this policy:

  • A purchase older than 7 days, counted as explained above.
  • A purchase of which any credit has been used, including credits used by automatic features.
  • A listing pack or one-off service of which any part has been delivered.
  • Credits that ended with a plan term.
  • A change of mind after any credit from the purchase has been used.
  • Results outside our control, such as a marketplace removing a listing or restricting an account, or a listing that does not sell.
  • Free, welcome, voucher and referral credits. No money was paid for them.
  • Part of a purchase. There are no partial refunds under this policy.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Write to billing@elmstool.com from the e-mail address of your ELMS account. You can also open a ticket in Support and choose the Billing topic.
  2. Say which purchase you mean. Give the invoice number or the date of the purchase. Do not send passwords or card numbers.
  3. We check the date and the credit use, and we reply by e-mail with our decision.
  4. If the refund is approved, it goes back to the payment method that was used, where our payment partner allows it. If it cannot go back to that method, our reply tells you what happens next. How long it takes to show on your statement depends on your bank or provider.

If you think a payment is wrong, write to billing@elmstool.com before you start a dispute with your bank or card issuer. The Terms explain what can happen to an account while a dispute is open.

After a refund

When we approve a refund:

  • the credits, the plan, the term and any extra eBay store allowance that came with that purchase end;
  • your account stays open;
  • free credits stay, and credits you had before the purchase are not affected;
  • a referral reward or affiliate commission that came from the purchase may be cancelled or reversed, as the Affiliate Terms explain.

We may refuse further refunds to an account that abuses refunds.

Stopping a renewal

If your payment method renews your plan, you can stop the renewal before the renewal date. Do it from your receipt, or write to billing@elmstool.com. Deleting your account does not stop a renewal, so stop it first.

Stopping a renewal does not refund a payment you already made. A refund follows the two conditions on this page.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any right you have by law that cannot be limited by contract. If you are a consumer, those rights may include a right to cancel within a statutory period and a remedy if the service is not as described. Nothing here is meant to remove them. To use them, write to billing@elmstool.com.
