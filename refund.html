Refund and Cancellation Policy

When you can get your money back for an ELMS plan, and what happens to credits when an action fails.

Last updated September 28, 2026

On this page

1. 7-day refund2. Failed actions3. Cancelling4. How to ask for a refund5. Your legal rights

17-day refund

You can ask for a full refund of a plan within 7 days of buying it, as long as none of the credits from that purchase have been used. We refund the full amount you paid to the payment method you used.

  • Credits count as used once an action has spent them, for example an import, a publish or an AI touch-up that succeeded.
  • If any credit from the purchase has been used, or the 7 days have passed, that purchase is not eligible for a refund under this rule.
  • Credits are a prepaid usage balance. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred.
Not sure whether your purchase qualifies? Write to us before the 7 days end and we will check your account.

2Failed actions

If an action fails because of a problem on our side, the credits it used are returned to your balance. You do not need to ask for this. A failed publish shows the reason on the listing.

3Cancelling

A plan is a single payment. It does not renew automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel and you will not be charged again unless you buy another plan yourself. You can stop using ELMS at any time.

4How to ask for a refund

  1. Write to billing@elmstool.com from the email address of your ELMS account.
  2. Tell us which plan you bought and when. If you have your receipt, attach it.
  3. We check that the purchase is inside the 7 days and that no credits from it were used, and we answer by email.
  4. If it is approved, the refund goes back to the original payment method. How long it takes to show on your statement depends on your bank or card provider.

5Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any right you have under the consumer law where you live. See also the Terms of Service (credits and payments), and the current prices on the Pricing page.

Questions: billing@elmstool.com for payments and refunds, support@elmstool.com for anything else.
