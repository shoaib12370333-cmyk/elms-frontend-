Refund Policy

When we refund

In brief

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of a purchase. You do not need to give a reason, and using credits does not stop a refund.

If you ask within 14 days of a purchase, we refund that purchase in full. This is true for every kind of purchase, and it does not depend on a reason or on whether you have used the credits that came with it. In full means everything you paid for that purchase, including any tax collected on it.

After 14 days, a purchase cannot be refunded under this policy. Your legal rights are not affected.

What happens in four common cases

How the 14 days are counted

In brief

The clock starts when your payment is confirmed. A request counts when it reaches support@elmstool.com, becomes a support ticket or is sent through the help link on your payment receipt.

Purchase date
The moment your payment was confirmed. We read it in UTC, the same way as on your invoice.
14 days
The day of the purchase and the 14 days after it, in UTC. A request counts until 23:59 UTC on the 14th day after the purchase.
A request counts
When it reaches support@elmstool.com, when it is created as a support ticket, or when you send it through the help link on your payment receipt (it counts from the day you sent it).

So for a payment confirmed at 14:30 UTC on the 3rd, a request that reaches us by 23:59 UTC on the 17th counts.

If you are not sure whether a purchase still qualifies, write to us before the 14 days end.

Send your request to support@elmstool.com or open a ticket in Support. A request sent to another ELMS address counts from the time it reaches support@elmstool.com or becomes a ticket. Our records of that time decide.

For a plan, your invoice shows the purchase date in UTC. We e-mail it after the payment, and you can download it again from Purchase history on the Buy Credits page. The invoice shows the day only. Purchase history shows the date and time in your own time zone. For a listing pack, the date is in Purchase history. For the exact moment of a purchase, ask support@elmstool.com.

Credits you have used

In brief

Credits you have used do not stop a refund. When a refund is approved, the credits from that purchase end.

A refund within 14 days is a full refund, even if you have already used some or all of the credits that came with the purchase. Credits can be used by you, by a person on your team or by an automatic feature such as stock and price monitoring. None of this changes the refund.

When we approve the refund, the credits from that purchase end, the used ones and the unused ones. Credits you had before the purchase, and free credits, are not affected.

Credits that were charged and then returned because an action failed are not a refund of money. See Failed actions.

Failed actions

In brief

If an action fails, its credits are returned to your balance automatically. You do not need to ask.

When an action does not complete, the credits it was charged are returned to your balance automatically, normally within moments. If you think a credit is missing, write to support@elmstool.com or open a ticket in Support, and we restore it.

Returned credits are not a refund of money. They go back into your balance.

Plans, listing packs and one-off services

The same rule applies to every kind of purchase. Each purchase has its own date and is counted on its own.

Plans and credits

A plan is paid for one term, and the credits that come with it are valid until the term ends. If you ask within 14 days of the purchase, the plan is refunded in full, whether or not you used credits.

Listing packs

A listing pack is delivered to your account as soon as the payment is confirmed. You can still ask for a refund within 14 days. When we approve it, we may remove the drafts that were delivered with that pack from your account.

One-off services

You can ask for a refund of a one-off service within 14 days of the purchase, also if the work has started or been delivered. When we approve it, we may remove what was delivered from your account.

What is not refundable

These cannot be refunded under this policy:

  • A purchase older than 14 days, counted as explained above.
  • Free, welcome, voucher and referral credits. No money was paid for them.
  • Part of a purchase. A refund is for the whole purchase, not for some of its credits or days.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Write to support@elmstool.com from the e-mail address of your ELMS account. You can also open a ticket in Support and choose the Billing topic.
  2. Say which purchase you mean. Give the invoice number or the date of the purchase. You do not need to give a reason. Do not send passwords or card numbers.
  3. We check that your request is within the 14 days. If it is, we approve it and tell you by e-mail.
  4. When we process the refund, it goes back to the payment method that was used, where our payment partner allows it. If it cannot go back to that method, our reply tells you what happens next. How long it takes to show on your statement depends on your bank or provider.

If you think a payment is wrong, write to support@elmstool.com before you start a dispute with your bank or card issuer. The Terms explain what can happen to an account while a dispute is open.

After a refund

When we approve a refund:

  • the credits, the plan, the term and any extra eBay store allowance that came with that purchase end;
  • your account stays open;
  • free credits stay, and credits you had before the purchase are not affected;
  • a referral reward or affiliate commission that came from the purchase may be cancelled or reversed, as the Affiliate Terms explain.

We may refuse a refund only where there is evidence of fraud, for example a stolen payment method, or of refunds being used again and again to get ELMS without paying. A refund you ask for in good faith within the 14 days is not refused.

Stopping a renewal

If your payment method renews your plan, you can stop the renewal before the renewal date. Do it from your receipt, or write to support@elmstool.com. Deleting your account does not stop a renewal, so stop it first.

Stopping a renewal does not refund a payment you already made. A refund follows the 14-day rule on this page, counted from the date of that payment.

Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any right you have by law that cannot be limited by contract. If you are a consumer, those rights may include a right to cancel within a statutory period and a remedy if the service is not as described. The 14 days in this policy are a minimum we give everybody. Nothing here is meant to remove your legal rights. To use them, write to support@elmstool.com.
