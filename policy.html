1Who we are

This policy explains what personal data ELMS collects, why, and the choices you have. ELMS is a tool for sellers that imports Amazon product information, creates eBay listings and manages related orders and messages.

The eBay permissions we ask for

When you connect a store, eBay shows you the permissions ELMS asks for. They are these, and we use each one only for the purpose next to it:

  • View public data from eBay: categories, item specifics and prices of similar items, to help you list.
  • View and manage your inventory and offers: to create, change and end your listings, and to read the rule problems eBay reports for them.
  • View and manage your account settings: to read your business policies (payment, shipping and returns) and your selling limits. We do not change your policies.
  • View and manage your order fulfillments: to read your orders and add tracking to them.
  • View your basic user information: your eBay username, to name the connection.
  • eBay message access: to read and answer your buyers’ messages.
  • View and manage your event notification subscriptions: to receive and check eBay’s notifications about orders and messages.

We do not ask for, and do not use, any permission for your marketing or ads, analytics reports, payments or refunds, payment disputes, feedback or reputation, returns, cancellations or cases, offers to buyers, or eBay Stores. You can disconnect your store at any time (see section 7).

3How we use it

  • To run ELMS: import products, publish and update listings, sync orders and messages, monitor stock and price.
  • To take payments and keep credit balances.
  • To secure the service and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send service emails about your account: the sign-up code, a welcome message, password and security alerts, receipts and invoices, and notices when your account is suspended, permanently banned or reinstated (with the reason, and with the message from our team when we reinstate an account).
  • To enforce our Terms: to suspend or permanently ban an account, to block an abusive network address or browser, and to read and answer appeals.
  • To look at what is in an account when it is needed, for example your drafts and the product links you import, so that we can give you support, look into abuse or keep ELMS safe. Our team does this only for those purposes.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use buyer data for advertising.

4Who we share it with

We share data only with services needed to run ELMS:

  • eBay — listings, order updates and replies you send are transmitted to eBay under your connected account.
  • Amazon product data provider — the product links or ASINs you import, to retrieve public product information.
  • Paddle — checkout, tax and payment processing.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • IP geolocation service — your IP address is sent to a public geolocation service (ipwho.is) to work out an approximate city and country for login activity and new-device alerts.
  • AI provider — only when you use the optional AI title suggestion: the product title and basic product details are sent to generate a suggestion.
  • Email delivery provider — the mail service that delivers our emails to you. It receives your email address and the text of the message.
  • Hosting and database providers — who store and process data for us under contract.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

5How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account is active.
  • Disconnecting an eBay store deletes the drafts, listings, orders, messages, notifications and import history ELMS stored for that store.
  • Messages moved to trash are removed after 30 days.
  • Login activity is kept for 180 days. A signed-in device record is removed when its session ends or expires (7 days).
  • When eBay tells us a user asked for their data to be deleted, we remove the related data.
  • Purchase records may be kept as long as needed for accounting and legal duties.
  • Records of a suspension or permanent ban, and the appeals that go with them, are kept as long as needed to enforce the decision, deal with disputes and meet legal duties. We may keep the record of a ban even if you ask us to delete your account, so that a banned account cannot simply come back.

6Security

Passwords are stored hashed, eBay authorization tokens are stored encrypted, and connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your accounts safe.

7Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent.

  • Edit your details and store names in the app, and disconnect a store to delete its data.
  • To ask for account deletion or a copy of your data, open Support in the app and send a ticket.
  • You can switch off news and offers with the unsubscribe link in those emails, and new-device sign-in alerts in Settings → Security. Service emails (codes, receipts, security alerts and notices about your account) are needed to run your account and stay on.
  • If your account was suspended, you can send an appeal from the blocked screen. A permanent ban cannot be appealed. See the Terms.
  • You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8Cookies and local storage

ELMS stores a session token and a few interface preferences in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in and pages remember your view. We do not use advertising cookies. Payment pages provided by Paddle and Google sign-in may set their own cookies under their own policies.

9Children

ELMS is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. For material changes we will tell you in the app or by email. For privacy questions open Support in the app and send a ticket.

Contact: support@elmstool.com for help, security@elmstool.com for account-security concerns, billing@elmstool.com for payments and credits, and hello@elmstool.com for anything else. Mails from noreply@elmstool.com are automatic; please do not reply to them.
